# 07 - Aristotle - Substance Causes Logic Ethics Politics.md

*Chapter context: Aristotle (384-322 BC), Plato's pupil for ~20 years, refutes Ideas and founds logic, biology and systematic philosophy.*

## 1. Life, Character, Project
- **Dates/background:** 384-322 BC; not native Athens; born Macedonia, came to Plato's Academy when Plato 61; pupil almost 20 years. Father respected physician → scientist; tells project: most interested in **nature study / natural processes**. Not only last great Greek philosopher, **Europe's first great biologist**.
- **Exaggerated contrast:** Plato so engrossed in eternal forms took little notice of changes in nature; Aristotle preoccupied with just these changes. Plato turned back on sensory world, shut eyes to escape cave to eternal ideas; Aristotle opposite: got down on all fours studied frogs/fish, anemones/poppies. Plato used reason; Aristotle used senses as well.
- **Writings/style:** Plato poet/mythologist; Aristotle dry/precise as encyclopedia, based on up-to-date field studies. Records refer to **170 titles**, **47 preserved**; not complete books, largely **lecture notes**; philosophy still mainly oral.
- **Significance:** created terminology scientists use today; great organizer who founded/classified various sciences. Wrote on all sciences.
- **Plan in book:** refutation of Ideas → own natural philosophy summing pre-Socratics → categories/logic → man/society.

## 2. Critique of Plato: No Innate Ideas, Forms in Things
- **Shared premises:** particular horse flows, no horse lives forever; actual form eternal/immutable.
- **Reversal:** Plato turned whole thing upside down. "Idea" horse simply **concept humans formed after seeing certain number of horses**. "Idea/form" horse = horse's characteristics defining what today call **species**. No existence of own.
- **Mold metaphor fails:** gingerbread mold exists independently of cookies. Aristotle disbelieved such molds/forms lying on shelf beyond natural world. Forms **in things**, as particular characteristics.
- **Chicken/egg:** disagreed idea chicken came before chicken. "Form" chicken present in every chicken as particular set characteristics — e.g., lays eggs. Real chicken and form chicken inseparable as **body/soul**.
- **Essence of criticism + dramatic turn:**
  - Highest reality for Plato = what think with reason. For Aristotle = what perceive with senses. Plato: sensory things purely reflections of higher reality in world of ideas/human soul. Aristotle opposite: things in human soul purely reflections of natural objects. **Nature is real world.** Plato trapped in mythical picture where imagination confused with real world.
  - **Empiricist principle:** nothing exists in consciousness that has not first been experienced by senses. Plato would say nothing in natural world not first existed in ideas. Aristotle: Plato **doubling number of things** — explains horse by idea horse; but where idea horse from? Might there even be third horse idea horse imitation of? (Third-man regress in plain form).
- **Innate debate:** all thoughts/ideas come via what heard/seen. But also **innate power of reason** — not innate ideas as Plato held, but innate faculty of organizing sensory impressions into **categories/classes**. Hence concepts "stone," "plant," "animal," "human"; "horse," "lobster," "canary."
  - Reason completely empty until sensed something. Man has no innate ideas. Reason most distinguishing characteristic.
- **Sophie's test (from questions):** newborn baby? Cannot imagine well equipped with ideas; no language ≠ no ideas, but must see things before know. Which came first chicken or idea chicken? Aristotle sides chicken.

## 3. Substance + Form, Potentiality/Actuality
- **Definition:** reality = various separate things constituting unity of **form + substance**. Substance = what things made of; form = each thing's specific characteristics / what it does.
- **Chicken:** form = precisely flutters, cackles, lays eggs = species characteristics. When dies/cackles no more, form ceases; only substance remains (sadly), but then no longer chicken.
- **Substance contains potentiality to realize specific form; strives toward innate potentiality.** Every change = transformation from **potential → actual**.
- **Sculptor story:** sculptor hacking formless granite daily; boy "What looking for?" "Wait and see." Days later beautiful horse carved; boy amazed: "How did you know it was in there?" In sense sculptor saw horse's form in block because block had potentiality to be formed into horse. Similarly everything in nature has potentiality of achieving specific form.
- **Egg:** chicken egg has potentiality to become chicken. Not all realize — many end breakfast as fried/omelette/scrambled without realizing potentiality. Equally obvious cannot become goose — that potentiality not within. Form says limitation as well as potentiality.
- **Nonliving:** not only organisms. As chicken's form to cackle/flutter/lay, stone's form to fall to ground. Chicken cannot help cackling, stone cannot help falling. Can lift/hurl high, but because nature to fall, cannot hurl to moon (take care: stone might take revenge find shortest route back!).
- **Scale preview:** nonliving no potentiality for (self) change, only via external influence; living have potentiality.

## 4. The Four Causes — Final Cause in Focus
- **Modern "cause" narrow:** how it came to happen. Window smashed because Peter hurled stone; shoe made because shoemaker sews leather. Aristotle held different types; **four causes**; important to understand **final cause**.
- **Purpose example:** window — reasonable ask why Peter threw stone = purpose. Shoe — purpose plays role. Aristotle also takes similar purpose for purely lifeless processes.
- **Rain — full four-cause analysis:**
  - School account Aristotle would nod to: rains because moisture in clouds cools/condenses into drops drawn by gravity.
  - But only three causes so far:
    - **Material cause:** moisture (clouds) was there at precise moment air cooled.
    - **Efficient cause:** moisture cools.
    - **Formal cause:** form/nature of water is to fall to earth.
  - If stop there Aristotle adds: rains **because plants/animals need rainwater to grow** = **final cause**. Assigns raindrops life-task/purpose.
  - Formula: oranges/grapes grow so people can eat; it rains so plants can grow.
- **Modern inversion:** we turn upside down: plants grow **because** find moisture. Food/water necessary conditions for man/beast; had we not had conditions would not exist. But not purpose of water/oranges to be food.
- **Assessment in book:** tempted to say Aristotle wrong. But not too hasty. Many believe God created world so creatures could live; viewed so can claim water in rivers because animals/humans need water. But now talking **God's purpose**; raindrops/waters have no interest in welfare. Distinguish natural teleology vs divine purpose.

## 5. Logic, Categories, Classification
- **Form/substance in discerning:** discern = classify in groups/categories. See horse, another, another; not exactly alike, but common something = horse's form; distinctive/individual = substance.
- **Pigeonholing:** cows in cowsheds, horses stables, pigs pigsties, chickens coops. Sophie tidying room: books bookshelf, schoolbooks schoolbag, magazines drawer; folds clothes closet — underwear one shelf, sweaters another, socks drawer. Same in minds: stone/wool/rubber; alive/dead; vegetable/animal/human.
- **Aristotle clearing nature's room:** everything belongs to different categories/subcategories. Example hierarchy: Hermes is live creature → animal → vertebrate → mammal → dog → Labrador → male Labrador.
- **Unclassifiable shock:** pick anything from floor, belongs to higher category; day see something unable to classify get shock. Discover small whatsit can't say animal/vegetable/mineral — dare not touch.
- **Party game:** victim sent outside, must guess what thinking of (Fluffy cat in neighbour's garden), only yes/no. Good Aristotelian game:
  Is it concrete? Yes! Mineral? No! Alive? Yes! Vegetable? No! Animal? Yes! Bird? No! Mammal? Yes! Whole animal? Yes! Cat? Yes! Fluffy? Yeah! Laughter.
  - Jokes: Aristotle invented that game; Plato hide-and-seek; Democritus Lego.
- **Founder of Logic:** meticulous organizer clarifying concepts; demonstrated laws governing valid conclusions/proofs. Example suffices:
  - Premise 1: all living creatures are mortal.
  - Premise 2: Hermes is a living creature.
  - Conclusion: Hermes is mortal. (**Syllogism**; correlation of terms "living creature"/"mortal").
  - 100% valid but hardly new (already knew Hermes mortal; dog→living→mortal unlike rock of Mount Everest). But class relations not always obvious; necessary to clarify.
  - **Mice example — thinking through:** Is it possible tiny baby mice suckle like lambs/piglets? Mice do not lay eggs (when last see mouse egg?) So bear live young like pigs/sheep. But animals bearing live young = **mammals** = feed on mother's milk. So got there; answer inside but had to think; forgot mice suckle because never seen — shy of humans when suckle.

## 6. Nature's Scale ( scala naturae )
- **First division:** nonliving vs living. Nonliving: stones, drops, soil — **no potentiality for change**, only via external influence. Only living have potentiality.
- **Living:** plants vs creatures; creatures → animals vs humans.
- **Clear/simple but question:** decisive difference rose vs stone, rose vs horse, horse vs man — what exactly difference consist of?
- **Criterion:** object's characteristics, what it can do/does:
  - All living (plants, animals, humans): absorb nourishment, grow, propagate.
  - All living creatures (animals+humans) plus: perceive world, move about.
  - All humans plus: think — order perceptions into categories/classes.
- **Continuum:** no sharp boundaries; gradual transition simple growths → complicated plants → simple → complicated animals. Top: **man** — lives whole life of nature: grows/absorbs like plants, feels/moves like animals, plus rational thought peculiar to humans.
- **Divine spark & God:** man has spark of divine reason. Must be God who started all movement; God at very top of scale. Imagined movement of stars/planets guiding all Earth movement; but something causing heavenly bodies to move = **first mover / God**. First mover itself at rest, but formal cause of heavenly movement, thus all nature movement.

## 7. Ethics: Happiness and Golden Mean
- **Return to man:** man's form comprises soul with plant-like, animal, rational parts. Question: how live? What to live good life? Answer: happiness only by using **all abilities/capabilities**.
- **Three forms of happiness (must co-present):**
  1. life of pleasure/enjoyment,
  2. life as free/responsible citizen,
  3. life as thinker/philosopher.
  All three same time for fulfilment. Rejected all imbalance. Today might say person only developing body just as unbalanced as only using head. Both extremes warped.
- **Golden Mean in relations:** neither cowardly nor rash but **courageous** (too little = cowardice, too much = rashness); neither miserly nor extravagant but **liberal**; same eating — dangerous too little or too much.
- **Medical echo:** both Plato and Aristotle ethics echo Greek medicine: only balance/temperance achieve happy/harmonious life.
- **Basics (from Sophie's reflection linked):** food, warmth, love, care; answers to philosophical questions; job liked (hate traffic → not taxi driver); not need lottery million; devil finds work for idle hands.

## 8. Politics: Man as Political Animal, Constitutions
- **Undesirability of extremes in society:** man by nature **political animal**. Without society not real people. Family/village satisfy primary needs food/warmth/marriage/child-rearing. Highest fellowship only in **state**.
- **How organize? (remember Plato's philosophic state):** three **good** forms:
  1. **Monarchy/kingship** — one head; must not degenerate into **tyranny** — one ruler governs to own advantage.
  2. **Aristocracy** — larger/smaller group rulers; beware degenerating into **oligarchy** — few run government (e.g., junta).
  3. **Polity** = democracy; negative aspect quickly develop into **mob rule** (even if tyrannic Hitler had not become head in Germany, lesser Nazis could form terrifying mob rule).
- **Function:** naming/categorizing nature (Sophie's formulation to mother: people live so someone can go around giving names; human thinking animal; if don't think not really human; without us none to tell cat/dog, lily/gooseberry).

## 9. Views on Women (as presented + Gaarder critique)
- **Not uplifting as Plato's:** inclined women incomplete — **unfinished man**. Reproduction: woman passive/receptive, man active/productive; child inherits only male characteristics; all characteristics complete in male sperm. Woman soil receiving/bringing forth seed, man sower. In Aristotelian language: man provides form, woman contributes substance.
- **Gaarder's meta-comment:** astonishing/regrettable otherwise intelligent man so wrong; demonstrates (1) could not have much practical experience of women/children, (2) how wrong when men reign supreme in philosophy/science.
- **Historical harm doubly:** his rather than Plato's view held sway Middle Ages; church inherited view entirely without foundation in Bible. Jesus certainly no woman hater!
