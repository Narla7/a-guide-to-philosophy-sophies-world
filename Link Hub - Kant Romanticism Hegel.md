## Flow diagram (text)

```
Enlightenment (reason / rights / mechanism)
  -> Kant (limit: das Ding an sich; ego contributes to cognition; aesthetics/play; duty)
    -> Romantic reaction (feeling, imagination, experience, yearning; ego-worship; genius; organism; world-soul; night/longing; national spirit; irony)
      -> Hegel system (Spirit / dialectic / history — gathering Romantic organism + Kant + Enlightenment into system)
```

- Enlightenment → Kant: cool intellectualism, limit of knowledge, freedom in play (*man is only free when he plays* — Schiller after Kant).
- Kant → Romanticism: successors exploiting ego-freedom; art brings closer to inexpressible / das Ding an sich; universe-creating imagination; Novalis dream/reality; Schelling Absolute; Fichte unconscious imagination; Herder organism/history.
- Romanticism → Hegel: world-spirit historicized/systematized; [[21 - Kierkegaard - Stages Subjectivity and Leap|Kierkegaard]] then reacts against Hegel system in turn.
