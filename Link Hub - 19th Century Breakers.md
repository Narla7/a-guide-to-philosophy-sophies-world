# Link Hub - 19th Century Breakers

Four unmaskers who break the system: existence, history, nature, psyche.

## Files

- [[21 - Kierkegaard - Stages Subjectivity and Leap|Kierkegaard]] — the single individual against Hegel's system; stages, leap, subjectivity
- [[22 - Marx - Alienation History and Capital|Marx]] — Hegel inverted: history as class struggle, alienation, capital
- [[23 - Darwin - Variation Struggle and Selection|Darwin]] — man naturalized from without: variation, struggle, selection
- [[24 - Freud - Unconscious Dreams and Repression|Freud]] — man naturalized from within: unconscious, dreams, repression

## Common thread

- Anti-Hegel/system: each rejects the closed rational system — Kierkegaard for existence, Marx for praxis, Darwin for contingency, Freud for the unconscious.
- Man re-described as natural, historical, existential being: embodied, class-bound, evolved, driven — no longer pure reason contemplating eternal Forms.
- Each practices unmasking: pious system-language hides interest (Marx), design (Darwin), or consciousness itself (Freud, Kierkegaard's crowd).
