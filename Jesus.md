# Jesus

*Split from [[09 - Two Cultures - Indo-Europeans Semites Jesus Paul|09 - Two Cultures]] §5 — full detail preserved. Overview kept in source file.*

## 1. Keywords retained — Messiah, Son of God, Kingdom of God
- "Messiah," "Son of God," "Kingdom of God" retained from Jewish background.
- At first all taken politically. Many at the time expected new "Messiah" as political/military/religious David-like national deliverer ending Roman suffering.
- Link to Israel background: kings chosen/anointed → title Messiah = "the anointed one"; king could be called "Son of God," country "Kingdom of God"; prophets after David's fall proclaim coming new king of House of David who redeems and founds "Kingdom of God."

## 2. Universal expectation
- More farsighted expectation: past 200 years prophets believed promised "Messiah" savior of whole world, not only Israelites from yoke, but all mankind from sin/blame/death.
- Longing for salvation/redemption widespread in Hellenistic world.

## 3. Jesus of Nazareth — message
- Not only claimant. Uses "Son of God," "Kingdom of God," "redemption" — maintains link to old prophets.
- Rides into Jerusalem acclaimed savior, playing on old kings' throne-accession ritual; allows anointing.
- Proclamation: **"The time is fulfilled, and the Kingdom of God is at hand."**

## 4. Decisive difference — no military/political rebel
- Clearly not military/political rebel. Mission much greater: salvation and forgiveness for everyone: **"Your sins are forgiven you for his name's sake."**

## 5. Unheard-of claims
- Handing "remission of sins" totally unheard; worse, addresses God as **"Father" (Abba)** — absolutely unprecedented in Jewish community then.
- Soon protest wave among scribes.

## 6. Reversed Kingdom
- Expected military flourish with trumpets/fire/sword; Jesus broader: Kingdom = loving neighbor, compassion for weak/poor, forgiveness of erring.
- Dramatic shift of warlike expression. In kirtle/sandals preaching new covenant **"love thy neighbor as thyself"** — plus love enemies, no retaliation, turn other cheek, forgive not seven but **seventy times seven**.

## 7. Practice
- Not above talking to harlots, corrupt usurers, politically subversive.
- Further: good-for-nothing squandering inheritance or humble publican pocketing funds is righteous before God when repents/prays — so great is mercy.
- Further still: such sinners more righteous/deserving forgiveness than spotless **Pharisees** flaunting virtue.

## 8. Grace vs merit — vs Greek self-redemption
- Nobody can earn mercy; cannot redeem ourselves (as many Greeks believed).
- Severe ethical demands in **Sermon on the Mount** both teach God's will and show no man righteous in God's eyes.
- Mercy boundless, but must turn/pray for forgiveness.

## 9. Fate
- Ingenious use of time's language giving old war cries new broader content.
- Not surprising ended on Cross — radical tidings at odds with interests/powers, had to be removed.

## 10. Socrates parallel — Passion
- Socrates showed dangerous appealing to reason; Jesus shows dangerous demanding unconditional brotherly love/forgiveness.
- Today mighty powers come apart confronted with peace, love, food for poor, amnesty for enemies of state.
- Plato incensed most righteous Athens forfeited life; Christians hold Jesus only righteous ever lived yet condemned.
- Died for humanity — **"Passion"**, "suffering servant" bearing sins that we be "atoned"/saved from wrath.
