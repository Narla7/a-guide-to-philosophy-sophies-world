# Link Hub - Enlightenment

*Hub for the French Enlightenment (file 17 split). Center of gravity in 18th century: **England first half, France middle, Germany toward end** — shift west to east. Dating anchors: Hume died 1776, about 20 years after Montesquieu, only 2 years before Voltaire and Rousseau (both 1778). All three had been to England, knew Locke. Slogan hung in cabin: **LIBERTY, EQUALITY AND FRATERNITY** / Family of Man letter (200 years ago united France, today should unite world; descendants/children inherit world).*

## The seven-point program (table)

| # | Point | Core |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opposition to authority | Skepticism to inherited truths; Descartes ground-up; England visit (Newton, Locke); against clergy, king, nobility; ideas predate 1789 |
| 2 | Rationalism | Age of Reason; Locke inconsistent empiricist (God/morals in reason); French "evident" vs British "common sense"; Socrates/Stoics parallel; morals/religion/ethics on immutable reason |
| 3 | Movement | Enlighten masses; poverty/oppression = ignorance/superstition; pedagogy founded (schools Middle Ages, pedagogy Enlightenment); Encyclopedia (see below) |
| 4 | Optimism | Reason/knowledge → progress; dominant till last decades; early criticism of "civilization" — "Maybe we should have listened" |
| 5 | Nature | Back to nature = reason (gift of nature); primitive peoples healthier/happier; [[Rousseau]]: man by nature good, civilization ruins; intrinsic value of childhood |
| 6 | Deism | Religion made natural, in harmony with reason; stripped of irrational dogmas; atheist materialists minority; world too rational (Newton); immortality as reason (Descartes); God created ages ago, no revelation since; "Supreme Being" via nature/laws only; parallel Aristotle formal cause / first mover |
| 7 | Rights | Practical fight for natural rights; censorship / freedom of press, thought/utterance; abolition of slavery; humane criminals; inviolability of individual; Declaration of the Rights of Man and Citizen 1789, basis Norway's Constitution 1814; natural rights by birth vs laws of land; women's rights → [[Olympe de Gouges]] (Condorcet 1787, Gouges 1791/1793) |

## Encyclopedia note

- Huge **Encyclopedia in 28 volumes, 1751–1772**. All great philosophers/men of letters contributed. "Everything is to be found here," from "way needles are made to way cannons are founded."
