# 20 - Hegel - Spirit, Dialectic and History

Context: Gaarder presents Hegel as legitimate child of Romanticism who salvages philosophy from dissolving into spirit by making reason historical and dialectical.

## 1. Life and System Character

- **Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel** legitimate child of Romanticism, developed with German spirit as gradually evolved.
- Born Stuttgart 1770, began study theology Tübingen age eighteen. Beginning 1799 worked with Schelling in Jena during Romantic Movement most explosive growth. After period assistant professor Jena became professor Heidelberg, center German National Romanticism. In 1818 appointed professor Berlin, just when city becoming spiritual center Europe. Died cholera 1831, but not before Hegelianism gained enormous following nearly all universities Germany. Covered lot ground - geography and ideas.
- United and developed almost all ideas surfaced Romantic period. But sharply critical many Romantics, including Schelling.
- Philosophy so all-embracing diversified that doubtful whether can say had own philosophy at all. Usually known as Hegel's philosophy mainly **method for understanding progress of history**. Teaches nothing about inner nature of life, but can teach think productively. Not unimportant.

## 2. World Spirit Re-defined: From Spooky to Human

- Schelling/other Romantics said deepest meaning lay in world spirit. Hegel also uses term world spirit / world reason, but new sense.
- Means **sum of human utterances, because only man has spirit.**
- Can speak progress of world spirit throughout history. Must never forget referring to human life, thought, culture.
- Makes spirit much less spooky. Not lying in wait like slumbering intelligence in rocks/trees.
- Critique of Kant's **das Ding an sich**: although Kant denied clear cognition innermost secrets, admitted exists unattainable truth. Hegel says **truth is subjective**, thus rejecting existence any truth above/beyond human reason. **All knowledge is human knowledge.** Had to get philosophers down to earth.

## 3. Reason as Historical: River Analogy

- All systems before Hegel had one thing common: attempt set up **eternal criteria** for what man can know. True Descartes, Spinoza, Hume, Kant. Each investigated basis human cognition, pronounced on timeless factor.
- Hegel did not believe possible. Basis human cognition **changed from one generation to next**. No eternal truths, no timeless reason. **Only fixed point philosophy can hold is history itself.**
- Objection: history constant change, how fixed? River also constant change. Doesn't mean can't talk about it. But cannot say at which place valley river is truest river. Just as much river all way through.
- History like running river. Every tiny movement water given spot determined by falls and eddies higher upstream. But also determined by rocks and bends at point observing.
- History of thought/reason same: thoughts washed along current past tradition, plus material conditions prevailing time, help determine how think. Can never claim particular thought correct for ever and ever. But thought can be correct from where stand.
- Not saying everything equally right/wrong, but some things right/wrong **in relation to certain historical context**:
  - Advocated slavery today at best thought foolish. Wouldn't considered foolish 2,500 years ago, even though already progressive voices abolition.
  - Local example: not more 100 years ago not unreasonable burn off large forest to cultivate land. Extremely unreasonable today. Completely different - and better - basis judgments.
- Philosophical reflection also: reason dynamic, process, and **truth is this same process**, since no criteria beyond historical process itself determine most true/reasonable.
- Cannot single out particular thoughts antiquity, Middle Ages, Renaissance, Enlightenment say right/wrong. Cannot say Plato wrong Aristotle right. Cannot say Hume wrong but Kant Schelling right. Antihistorical way thinking.
- Cannot detach philosopher/thought from historical context.
- But - because something new always added, reason **progressive**. Human knowledge constantly expanding/progressing.
- Is Kant nevertheless more right than Plato? Yes. World spirit developed - progressed - Plato to Kant. Good thing! Return river: now more water, running over thousand years. Only Kant shouldn't think his truths will remain banks like immovable rocks. Kant's ideas get processed too, reason becomes subject future generations criticism. Exactly what happened.

## 4. Goal: Spirit Coming to Consciousness

- Where river go? Hegel: world spirit developing toward ever-expanding knowledge of itself. Same rivers become broader nearer sea.
- History story of world spirit gradually coming to consciousness of itself. Although world always existed, human culture/development made world spirit increasingly conscious intrinsic value.
- How sure? Claimed as historical reality, not prediction. Anybody studies history will see humanity advanced toward ever-increasing self-knowledge and self-development. Study shows moving toward greater rationality and freedom. Despite all capers, development progressive. Say history purposeful. Develops clear enough.

## 5. Dialectic: Thesis - Antithesis - Synthesis (Full Examples)

- Yes history one long chain reflections. Certain rules apply. Anyone studying depth observe thought usually proposed basis other previously proposed thoughts. As soon one proposed, contradicted by another. Tension arises between two opposite ways thinking. Resolved by proposal third which accommodates best both points view. Calls **dialectic process**.
- Not pressing history into framework. Believed history itself revealed dialectical pattern. Claimed uncovered laws development reason / progress world spirit through history.
- Hegel dialectic not only history. When discuss something, think dialectically. Try find flaws argument. Called **negative thinking**. But when find flaws, preserve best.

### Example A: Greeks on change
- Pre-Socratics discussed primeval substance and change.
- **Thesis:** Eleatics claimed change impossible. Forced deny change even though register changes senses.
- **Antithesis / negation:** Heraclitus - everything flows. Tension diametrically opposed.
- **Synthesis / negation of negation:** Empedocles pointed both partly right partly wrong.
  - Eleatics right nothing actually changes, not right holding cannot rely senses.
  - Heraclitus right can rely senses, not right holding everything flows.
  - Reason: more than one substance. Combination flowed, not substance itself.
- Terrible term negation of negation, but means compromise preserving truth.

### Example B: Rationalism - Empiricism - Kant
- Descartes rationalism thesis - contradicted by Hume empirical antithesis. Contradiction/tension resolved Kant synthesis. Kant agreed rationalists some things empiricists others.
- Story doesn't end Kant. Kant synthesis now point departure another chain reflections / triad. Because synthesis also contradicted by new antithesis.

### Example C: Discussion / politics
- Socialist and conservative sit resolve social problem, tension quickly revealed conflicting modes thought. Does not mean one absolutely right other totally wrong. Possible both partly right partly wrong. As argument evolves, best both often crystallize.
- While throes discussion, not easy decide more rational. In way up to history decide right/wrong.
> The reasonable is that which is viable.
> Whatever survives is right. Or vice versa: that which is right survives.

### Example D: Women's rights and hindsight
- 150 years ago lot fighting women's rights. Many bitterly opposed equal rights. Read arguments both sides today, not difficult see which more reasonable. Must not forget have hindsight. Proved case those fought equality right. Lot would cringe if saw print what grandfathers said.
- Hegel own view? Quote:
> The difference between man and woman is like that between animals and plants. Men correspond to animals, while women correspond to plants because their development is more placid and the principle that underlies it is the rather vague unity of feeling. When women hold the helm of government, the state is at once in jeopardy, because women regulate their actions not by the demands of universality but by arbitrary inclinations and opinions. Women are educated--who knows how?--as it were by breathing in ideas, by living rather than by acquiring knowledge. The status of manhood, on the other hand, is attained only by the stress of thought and much technical exertion.
- Striking example how views of rational change all time. Shows Hegel also child of time. So are we. Our obvious views will not stand test time either.
- No examples of our future error? Because would exemplify things already undergoing change. Could say stupid drive car because pollute. Lots already think. But history will prove much obvious will not hold light history.
- Dialectical twist: many men who reeled gross broadsides inferiority hastened feminism. Proposed thesis. Why? Because women already begun rebel. No need opinion something everyone agrees. More grossly expressed inferiority, stronger became negation.
- Best can happen energetic opponents. More extreme become, more powerful reaction face. More grist to mill.

### Example E: Pure logic - Being / Nothing / Becoming
- From pure logic/philosophy often dialectical tension two concepts.
- Reflect concept **being**, obliged introduce opposite **nothing**. Can't reflect existence without immediately realizing won't always exist. Tension being/nothing resolved in **becoming**. Because if something in process becoming, both is and is not.
- Reason thus dynamic logic. Since reality characterized opposites, description reality must therefore also full opposites.

### Example F: Bohr, profound truths
- Danish physicist Niels Bohr told story Newton's horseshoe over front door for luck. Only superstition, Newton anything but superstitious. Asked if really believed, said *No, I don't, but I'm told it works anyway.* Quite dialectical, contradiction almost. Bohr, like Norwegian poet Vinje, known ambivalence, once said:
> There are two kinds of truths. There are the superficial truths, the opposite of which are obviously wrong. But there are also the profound truths, whose opposites are equally right.
- If say life short, agree. But another occasion throw open arms say life long. Also true in sense.

### Example G: Deadlock and sudden change
- Imagine young girl always answers mother Yes, Mom ... Okay, Mom ... As you wish, Mom ... At once, Mom. Shudders.
- Finally mother absolutely maddened overobedience, shouts Stop being such goody-goody! Girl answers Okay, Mom. Would slap.
- What if answered instead But I won't to be goody-goody? Odd answer. Maybe slap anyway.
- Situation deadlocked. Dialectic tension come point where something had to happen. Final aspect leads sudden change / spontaneous act.

## 6. Objective Spirit: Family, Civil Society, State

- Remember Romantics individualists - path mystery leads inwards. This individualism met negation/opposite in Hegel.
- Emphasized **objective powers**. Among such: family, civil society, state. Somewhat skeptical individual. Believed individual organic part community. Reason / world spirit came to light first foremost interplay people.
- Reason manifests above all language. Language something born into. Norwegian language manages quite well without Mr. Hansen, but Mr. Hansen cannot manage without Norwegian. Thus not individual forms language, language forms individual.
- Same baby born into language, also born into historical background. Nobody has free relationship to background. He who does not find place within state therefore unhistorical person. Central also great Athenian philosophers. Just as state unthinkable without citizens, citizens unthinkable without state. Obviously.
- State more than individual citizen. Moreover more than sum citizens. Cannot resign from society. Anyone simply shrugs shoulders society live in and wants find soul will therefore be ridiculed.
- According Hegel, not individual finds itself, world spirit finds itself. (Master-slave dialectic not detailed in this passage.)

## 7. Three Stages of Spirit Returning to Itself

- World spirit returns to itself in three stages - becomes conscious itself:

1. **Subjective spirit:** first becomes conscious in individual.
2. **Objective spirit:** reaches higher consciousness in family, civil society, state. Called objective because appears interaction between people.
3. **Absolute spirit:** highest self-realization in **art, religion, and philosophy**. Of these philosophy highest form knowledge because in philosophy world spirit reflects on own impact on history. World spirit first meets itself in philosophy. Could say philosophy mirror of world spirit. Sophie liked last bit / beautiful.

### Rational is real
- Chapter epigraph in book: *the reasonable is that which is viable* - variant of famous Hegel formula *What is rational is real and what is real is rational [Das Vernünftige ist wirklich]*. Gaarder renders as viable/survives test history. No separate thing-in-itself beyond reason.
