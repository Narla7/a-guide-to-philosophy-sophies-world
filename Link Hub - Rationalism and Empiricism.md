## Rationalism vs Empiricism

| | Rationalism | Empiricism |
|---|---|---|
| Source of knowledge | Reason / innate ideas | Senses / experience |
| Model | Mathematics (Descartes, Spinoza's geometry) | Observation, habit (Locke's tabula rasa, Hume's bundle) |
| God/matter | Proved by reason; substance debates | Locke: residue of reason; Berkeley: spirit only; Hume: agnostic habit |
| Morals | Natural right in reason (Socrates to Locke) | Hume: feelings, is-ought gap |
| Crisis | Dogmatic system | Skepticism (causality, self) |
