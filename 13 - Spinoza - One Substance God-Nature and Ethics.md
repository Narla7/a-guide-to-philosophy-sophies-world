# 13 – Spinoza: One Substance, God-Nature, and Ethics

*Context: Baruch Spinoza (1632–1677), Descartes's great successor, rejects dualism for monism – God is Nature – and builds a geometrically demonstrated ethics of freedom as understanding necessity.*

## 1. Life – Excommunication and Lens-Grinder

- **Baruch Spinoza 1632–1677,** Jewish community Amsterdam, excommunicated for heresy; deserted even by family, who tried to disinherit him.
- Among most blasphemed/persecuted recent philosophers because he criticized established religion: Christianity and Judaism kept alive by rigid dogma and outer ritual.
- First to apply **historico-critical Bible interpretation:** Bible not inspired by God down to last letter; must read bearing in mind period of writing; critical reading reveals inconsistencies.
- Beneath New Testament surface: Jesus as God's mouthpiece, preaching "religion of reason" valuing love highest – love of God + love of humanity; liberation from Jewish orthodoxy. But Christianity itself hardened into dogma/ritual.
- Response to persecution: quiet secluded life devoted to philosophy; meager living polishing lenses (Alberto owns some – extremely costly treasures). Symbolic: philosopher helps people see life in new perspective; pillar of Spinoza = seeing things *from perspective of eternity.*
- Powerful champion of free speech and religious tolerance precisely because opposed on all sides.
- Main work: ***Ethics Geometrically Demonstrated.*** *Ethics* = art of living + moral conduct (Socrates/Aristotle sense, not modern "don't tread on toes" rules).

## 2. Against Descartes – From Two Substances to One

- Descartes: reality = two separate substances, thought + extension. "Substance" = what something consists of / reducible to.
- Spinoza rejects split: **only one Substance.** Everything reducible to single reality called Substance, at times God or Nature. **Monist** (vs. Descartes dualist).
- Difference less deep than claimed in one respect: Descartes also said only God exists independently. Spinoza's break: **identifying God with nature/creation** – departing from Jewish-Christian doctrine and Descartes.
- Formula: **Deus sive Natura – God or Nature; God is all, all is in God; world is in God** (quoting Paul on Areopagos: "In him we live and move and have our being"). God did not create world to stand outside it; God *is* world.
- "Nature" here = not only extended nature but everything existing, including spiritual.

## 3. Substance, Attributes, Modes

- We humans recognize two of God's qualities/manifestations – **attributes:** Thought and Extension (identical to Descartes's two substances, now demoted to attributes of one Substance). God may have infinitely more attributes, but only these two known to man.
- Everyday phenomena = **modes:** particular manners Substance/God/Nature assumes. Flower = mode of extension; poem about flower (e.g. Wordsworth) = mode of thought; both expressions of one Substance.
- Example: stomach pain – who has pain? Me. Recollection of pain – who thinks? Me. One person pained one minute, thoughtful next. All material happenings + all thoughts are God's/Nature's expression. Everything is One; one God, one Nature, one Substance.
- Lens metaphor: you may say *you* think/move, or say *nature* thinks through you / moves through you – which lenses you look through. You are Sophie Amundsen but also expression of infinitely bigger; "finger of God's body."

## 4. God Not Puppeteer – Inner Cause and Determinism

- Thumb example: you may move thumb as you choose, but only according to its nature – cannot jump off hand and dance. You have place in structure of existence.
- God/Nature = **inner cause,** not outer cause, speaking only through laws of nature:
  - Puppeteer = outer cause, pulls strings from outside.
  - God controls world through natural laws from within; everything material happens through **necessity.** Determinist view of natural world.
- Illustrations of non-freedom:
  - Stone Age boy 30,000 years ago in Rhine valley: cast spears, loved woman, worshipped tribal gods – did he decide all that?
  - Lion in Africa: does it decide to be beast of prey, attack limping antelope, or be vegetarian? Obeys nature/laws.
  - Newborn screaming for milk, sucking thumb – free will? Age two running/pointing, three nagging, four fearing dark, fifteen experimenting with makeup – where is freedom? So many complex hidden reasons we don't realize law-governed.
  - Two apple trees same age: sunny good soil/water vs. dark poor soil – bigger/more fruit obviously first. That tree is **free** in Spinoza's sense: full freedom to develop inherent abilities (though apple cannot bear pears). Humans similarly: hindered by politics/outer conditions, inner potential + outer opportunities determine growth, like boy, lion, tree.
- Only being totally "its own cause," acting with complete freedom in nonaccidental process: **God/Nature.** Man can strive to live without outer constraint, develop innate abilities, but never achieves "free will." We don't control bodily processes (mode of extension) nor "choose" thinking; no "free soul" – soul more or less imprisoned in mechanical body.

## 5. Critique of Anthropomorphic God and Bible

- Anthropomorphic, providential, miracle-working God rejected; God = eternal necessary order, not external ruler rewarding/punishing or intervening.
- Historico-critical method exposes human, time-bound Bible; dogma/ritual obscure Jesus's rational religion of love.
- Hence charge of pantheism/heresy: **pantheist** – God = all.

## 6. Ethics – Passions, Reason, Freedom

- Geometrical method: like Descartes's mathematical ideal, strictly logical conclusions; human life subject to universal laws of nature; must free selves from feelings/passions to find contentment/happiness.
- **Passions** (ambition, lust, etc.) prevent true happiness/harmony. Stoic resonance: Stoics also said everything by necessity, meet with stoicism, don't get carried away.
- Path: recognize everything happens from necessity → achieve intuitive understanding of nature as whole → realize with crystal clarity everything related, everything One → comprehend all in all-embracing perception → true happiness/contentment.
- Freedom redefined: not free will but acting from understanding, developing own nature unhindered; free tree/person = realizing inherent abilities within necessity.
- Goal formula: ***sub specie aeternitatis* – seeing everything from perspective of eternity / under aspect of eternity.**
  - Exercise: imagine own life cosmically – minuscule part of nature's life, ripple on endless sea; no difference between you and Rhine-valley boy 30,000 years ago except you live now; who will you be in 30,000 years? Perceive all nature/universe at single glance, infinity of space + eternity of time.
  - Lenses help see eternity; polishing lenses = learning to see so.

> Quotes: "...God is not a puppeteer..." / "seeing everything 'sub specie aeternitatis' – from the perspective of eternity." / "God is all, and all is in God." / "In him we live and move and have our being."
