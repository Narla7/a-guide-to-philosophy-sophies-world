# Schelling

*Context: German Romanticism, leading philosopher of Nature-philosophy. Reaction to Enlightenment mechanistic universe; renaissance of old cosmic consciousness. Roots: Spinoza, Plotinus, Jakob Böhme, Giordano Bruno — divine ego in nature, pantheists.*

## 1. Absolute / world spirit

- **Schelling (1775–1854)**: wanted unite mind and matter. All nature — human soul and physical reality — expression of one Absolute, or world spirit.
- Nature and spirit actually expressions same thing. Saw same world spirit in nature and human mind.
- World spirit can thus be sought both in nature and own mind. [[Novalis]]: ***the path of mystery leads inwards.***

## 2. Nature-philosophy formulas

- *Nature is visible spirit, spirit is invisible nature*, since senses structuring spirit everywhere.
- *Matter is slumbering intelligence.*
- Descartes and Hume had drawn sharp line between ego and extended reality. Kant left sharp distinction between cognitive I and nature in itself. Now said **nature is nothing but one big I**. Also used expressions **world soul / world spirit**.

## 3. Organism vs mechanism

- Characteristic Romantic view: **nature as organism**, unity constantly developing innate potentialities. Like flower unfolding leaves/petals. Or poet unfolding verses.
- Reminds Aristotle: Aristotelian as well as Neoplatonic overtones. Aristotle more organic view than mechanical materialists.
- For many, philosophy, nature study, poetry formed synthesis. Sitting attic dashing inspired verses and investigating life plants or composition rocks only two sides same coin because nature not dead mechanism, one living world spirit.
- Norwegian-born naturalist **Henrik Steffens** lecture Copenhagen 1801: *Tired of eternal efforts to fight our way through raw matter, we chose another way and sought to embrace the infinite. We went inside ourselves and created a new world...*
- Schelling saw development in nature from earth and rock to human mind. Very gradual transitions inanimate to complicated life forms.

## 4. Dark God

- **Schelling** said explicitly world is in God. God aware some of it, but other aspects represent unknown in God. For **God also has dark side**. Fascinating frightening, reminds Berkeley.
- Sum-up: world soul as ego which in more/less dreamlike state created everything. [[Fichte]] said nature stems from higher, unconscious imagination.
- Preoccupation with dark side of life, murky, uncanny, mystical belongs to same Romantic mood.

## Quotes

- Nature is visible spirit, spirit is invisible nature. / Matter is slumbering intelligence.
