# 09 - Two Cultures: Indo-Europeans, Semites, Jesus, Paul

*Context: European civilization roots in both Indo-European (Greek/Roman) and Semitic (Jewish/Christian) cultures, whose collision creates Christianity and ends antiquity.*

## 1. Prelude: Recycling and a New Savior

- Hellenistic philosophers recycled predecessors, some turning them into religious prophets — **Plotinus near-acclaimed Plato as savior of humanity**.
- But another savior born during same period outside Greco-Roman area: **Jesus of Nazareth**. Christianity gradually permeates Greco-Roman world.
- Thesis: Jesus a Jew = Semitic culture; Greeks/Romans = Indo-European. Europe rooted in both. Must examine roots before seeing Christian influence.

## 2. The Indo-Europeans

- **Definition:** All nations/cultures using Indo-European languages — all European except Finno-Ugrian (Lapp, Finnish, Estonian, Hungarian) and Basque; plus most Indian and Iranian languages.
- **Migrations (~4,000 years ago):** Primitive Indo-Europeans around Black Sea/Caspian Sea; waves southeast to Iran/India, southwest to Greece/Italy/Spain, west through Central Europe to France/Britain, northwest to Scandinavia, north to Eastern Europe/Russia. Assimilated local culture, but language and religion dominant.
- **Language → ideas:** Vedic scriptures, Greek philosophy, Snorri Sturluson's mythology written in related languages; related languages lead to related ideas → Indo-European "culture."
- **Polytheism and god-names (evidence):**
  - Celestial god **Dyaus** (Sanskrit sky/day/heaven) = **Zeus** (Greek) = **Jupiter** (Latin *iov-pater*, Father Heaven) = **Tyr** (Old Norse) — dialectal variants of same word.
  - Vikings' **Aser** = **asura** (Sanskrit) = **Ahura** (Persian).
  - "God": **deva** (Sanskrit) = **daeva** (Persian) = **deus** (Latin) = **tivurr** (Old Norse).
  - Fertility group **vaner** (Niord, Freyr, Freyja) related to **Venus** (Latin fertility goddess) and **van** (Sanskrit "desire").
- **Myths:** Affinity in myths — Snorri's Norse myths similar to Indian myths handed down 2–3,000 years earlier. Nordic vs Indian environment, but common origin traces clearest in immortal potions and gods vs monsters of chaos.
- **Thought-style — Good vs Evil drama:** World as drama where Good and Evil confront in relentless struggle; Indo-Europeans often try to "predict" outcome of battles.
- **Speculative tendency:** No accident Greek philosophy originates Indo-European sphere. Indian, Greek, Norse mythology lean to philosophic/speculative worldview.
- **Sight as supreme sense; "insight" etymology:**
  - Sanskrit **vidya** (insight/knowledge) = Greek **idea** (central to Plato) = Latin **video** (on Roman ground simply "to see"). "I see" = "I understand" (Woody Woodpecker light bulb); English **wise/wisdom**, German **wissen**, Norwegian **viten** — same root.
  - Literature of Indians, Greeks, Persians, Teutons characterized by great cosmic **visions** (*vision* from *video*). Characteristic to make pictures/sculptures of gods and mythical events.
- **Cyclical history:** History goes in circles like seasons. No beginning/end, but civilizations rise/fall in eternal birth/death interplay.
- **Hinduism/Buddhism parallels:**
  - Both Oriental religions Indo-European origin; clear parallels to Greek philosophy; strongly imbued with reflection even today.
  - Emphasis deity present in all things (**pantheism**); man can become one with God through insight (cf. Plotinus). Requires deep self-communion/meditation. Passivity/seclusion religious ideals. In Greece too many believed ascetic/secluded life for salvation; medieval monastic life traces to Greco-Roman beliefs.
  - **Transmigration / cycle of rebirth** fundamental in many Indo-European cultures. 2,500+ years ultimate Indian purpose = release from cycle. Plato also believed transmigration.

## 3. The Semites

- **Origin:** Completely different culture/language. Originated Arabian Peninsula, migrated; Jews lived far from home 2,000+ years. History/religion reached furthest via Christendom, also widely spread via Islam.
- **Three Western religions:** Judaism, Christianity, Islam share Semitic background. Koran and Old Testament written in Semitic languages. Old Testament word for "god" same semantic root as Muslim **Allah** ("allah" simply means "god").
- **Christian complication:** Also Semitic background, but New Testament written in Greek; theology/creed formulated influenced by Greek/Latin, thus Hellenistic philosophy.
- **Monotheism:** Vs Indo-European many gods, Semites united earliest in belief in **one God** — monotheism. Shared fundamental idea of all three.
- **Linear history:** History as ongoing line: beginning God created world; end Judgment Day judging living/dead. Role of history central: God intervenes; history exists that God manifests will (as led Abraham to Promised Land, leads mankind to Judgment when evil destroyed).
- **Historiography:** Preoccupied writing history thousands of years; historical roots very core of holy scriptures.
- **Jerusalem:** Significant center for all three — synagogues, churches, mosques. Tragic bone of contention, killing by thousands over ascendancy of "Eternal City." Hope UN makes it holy shrine for all three.
- **Hearing as supreme sense:** As interesting as Indo-European sight. Jewish creed begins **"Hear, O Israel!"** Old Testament people "heard" word of Lord; prophets begin **"Thus spake Jehovah (God)."** Hearing emphasized in Christianity; ceremonies of all three characterized by reading aloud/"reciting."
- **No images:** Vs Indo-European pictorial sculpture, Semites characteristically never made them. Old Testament commands no image of God; still law in Judaism and Islam. Islam general aversion to photography/art — not compete with God in "creating."
  - Apparent exception — Christian churches full of Jesus/God pictures — is Greco-Roman influence. Greek Orthodox (Greece, Russia) still forbids "graven images"/sculptures/crucifixes from Bible stories.
- **Distance + redemption vs release:** Vs Oriental religions, three Western emphasize distance between God and creation. Purpose not release from rebirth but **redeemed from sin and blame**. Religious life = prayer, sermons, study of scriptures more than self-communion/meditation.

## 4. Israel — Jewish Background of Christianity

- **Creation to Fall:** Began God created world (first Bible page). Mankind rebels; punishment not only Adam/Eve driven from Eden — Death enters world. Disobedience runs through Bible.
- **Flood to Covenant:** Genesis Flood/Noah's Ark. God makes **covenant/pact with Abraham and seed**: keep Lord's commandments, in exchange protection. Renewed when **Moses given Ten Commandments on Mount Sinai ~1200 B.C.** Israelites long slaves in Egypt, with God's help led back to Israel.
- **Kings (~1,000 B.C., long before Greek philosophy):** Three great kings — **Saul, David, Solomon**. All Israelites united one kingdom; under David especially political/military/cultural glory.
- **Messiah theology:** Kings chosen anointed → title **Messiah = "the anointed one."** Religiously go-between God/people. King could be called **"Son of God,"** country **"Kingdom of God."**
- **Division and catastrophes:** Power lost, divided Northern (Israel) / Southern (Judea). **722 B.C.** Northern conquered Assyrians, loses political/religious significance. Southern conquered **Babylonians 586 B.C.**, temple destroyed, most carried to Babylon — **Babylonian captivity till 539 B.C.**, permitted return, temple restored. Rest before Christ under foreign domination.
- **Theodicy question:** Constantly asked why David's Kingdom destroyed, catastrophes rained though God promised to hold Israel. Answer widely accepted: God punishing disobedience (people promised commandments).
- **Prophets — two types:**
  - From ~750 B.C. prophets preach God's wrath for not keeping commandments; Day of Judgment over Israel → **Doomsday prophecies**.
  - Later prophets preach God will redeem chosen few, send **"Prince of Peace" / king of House of David**, restore old Kingdom, future prosperity → **prophecies of redemption**. Isaiah: *"The people that walked in darkness will see a great light... they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined."*
- **Summary formula:** Happily under David; deterioration → prophets proclaim coming new king of House of David — "Messiah"/"Son of God" who redeems, restores greatness, founds "Kingdom of God."

## 5. Jesus

- **Keywords retained:** "Messiah," "Son of God," "Kingdom of God." At first all taken politically. Many at time expected new "Messiah" as political/military/religious David-like national deliverer ending Roman suffering.
- **Universal expectation:** More farsighted: past 200 years prophets believed promised "Messiah" savior of whole world, not only Israelites from yoke, but all mankind from sin/blame/death. Longing for salvation/redemption widespread Hellenistic world.
- **Jesus of Nazareth:** Not only claimant. Uses "Son of God," "Kingdom of God," "redemption" — maintains link old prophets. Rides into Jerusalem acclaimed savior, playing on old kings' throne-accession ritual; allows anointing. **"The time is fulfilled, and the Kingdom of God is at hand."**
- **Decisive difference:** Clearly not military/political rebel. Mission much greater: salvation and forgiveness for everyone: **"Your sins are forgiven you for his name's sake."**
- **Unheard-of claims:** Handing "remission of sins" totally unheard; worse, addresses God as **"Father" (Abba)** — absolutely unprecedented Jewish community then. Soon protest wave among scribes.
- **Reversed Kingdom:** Expected military flourish trumpets/fire/sword; Jesus broader: Kingdom = loving neighbor, compassion weak/poor, forgiveness erring. Dramatic shift of warlike expression. Kirtle/sandals preaching new covenant **"love thy neighbor as thyself"** — plus love enemies, no retaliation, turn other cheek, forgive not seven but **seventy times seven**.
- **Practice:** Not above talking to harlots, corrupt usurers, politically subversive. Further: good-for-nothing squandering inheritance or humble publican pocketing funds is righteous before God when repents/prays — so great mercy. Further still: such sinners more righteous/deserving forgiveness than spotless **Pharisees** flaunting virtue.
- **Grace vs merit:** Nobody can earn mercy; cannot redeem ourselves (as many Greeks believed). Severe ethical demands in **Sermon on the Mount** both teach God's will and show no man righteous in God's eyes. Mercy boundless, but must turn/pray forgiveness.
- **Fate:** Ingenious use of time's language giving old war cries new broader content. Not surprising ended on Cross — radical tidings at odds with interests/powers, had to be removed.
- **Socrates parallel:** Socrates showed dangerous appealing to reason; Jesus shows dangerous demanding unconditional brotherly love/forgiveness. Today mighty powers come apart confronted with peace, love, food for poor, amnesty for enemies of state. Plato incensed most righteous Athens forfeited life; Christians hold Jesus only righteous ever lived yet condemned. Died for humanity — **"Passion"**, "suffering servant" bearing sins that we be "atoned"/saved from wrath.

## 6. Paul and the Early Church

- **Resurrection foundation:** Days after crucified/buried, rumors risen from grave — proved no ordinary man, truly "Son of God." Church founded Easter Morning. Paul: **"And if Christ be not risen, then is our preaching vain and your faith is also vain."**
- **Resurrection of body vs immortal soul:** Now mankind can hope resurrection of body; crucified to save us. Jewish view: no "immortality of soul" or "transmigration" — that Greek/Indo-European thought. Christianity: nothing in man — no "soul" — in itself immortal. Church believes "resurrection of the body and eternal life" by God's miracle saving from death/"damnation" — neither own merit nor natural/innate ability.
- **Glad tidings:** Early Christians preach salvation through faith in Jesus Christ; mediation makes Kingdom about reality; entire world can be won for Christ ("christ" = Greek translation of Hebrew "messiah," anointed one).
- **Paul's mission:** Few years after death, Pharisee Paul converts; many missionary journeys across Greco-Roman world make it worldwide religion (Acts of Apostles; Epistles to congregations).
- **Paul in Athens (Areopagos):** Wanders into marketplace/philosophic capital; spirit stirred seeing city wholly idolatry. Visits synagogue, converses Epicurean/Stoic philosophers. Taken to Areopagos hill: *"May we know what this new doctrine... thou bringest certain strange things to our ears..."* Jew speaking of crucified/risen savior — coming collision Greek philosophy vs redemption — yet Athenians listen. Beneath Acropolis temples speech (Acts):
  > "Ye men of Athens, I perceive that in all things ye are too superstitious. For as I passed by, and beheld your devotions, I found an altar with this inscription, TO THE UNKNOWN GOD. Whom therefore ye ignorantly worship, him declare I unto you. God that made the world and all things therein, seeing that he is Lord of heaven and earth, dwelleth not in temples made with hands; neither is worshipped with men's hands, as though he needed any thing, seeing he giveth to all life, and breath, and all things... that they should seek the Lord... For in him we live, and move, and have our being; as certain also of your own poets have said, For we are also his offspring... we ought not to think that the Godhead is like unto gold, or silver, or stone, graven by art and man's device... but now commandeth all men everywhere to repent: Because he hath appointed a day, in the which he will judge the world in righteousness by that man whom he hath ordained; whereof he hath given assurance unto all men, in that he hath raised him from the dead."
- **Interpretation:** Christianity penetrates as something else, completely different from Epicurean/Stoic/Neoplatonic philosophy. Yet common ground: search for God natural to all — not new to Greeks. New: God revealed himself, reached out — no longer "philosophic God" approachable by understanding, nor image gold/silver/stone (plenty on Acropolis/marketplace). Personal God intervening history, dying on Cross for mankind.
- **Response:** Some mock resurrection; others "We will hear thee again"; some follow/believe — notably woman **Damaris** (women among most fervent converts). Mission continues; decades after death congregations in Athens, Rome, Alexandria, Ephesos, Corinth; in 300–400 years entire Hellenistic world Christian.

## 7. The Creed — Synthesis with Hellenism

- **Paul as organizer:** Not only missionary but guidance within congregations needing spiritual direction.
- **Universalism question:** Could non-Jews become Christians without first becoming Jews — e.g. Greek observe dietary laws? Paul: unnecessary. Christianity more than Jewish sect; universal salvation message. **"Old Covenant" God-Israel replaced by "New Covenant" Jesus established God-mankind.**
- **Need for dogma:** Not only religion then; Hellenistic fusion required church concise summary to distance from other religions and prevent schisms → first **Creed** summing central dogmas/tenets.
- **Central tenet — fully God, fully man:** Jesus both God and man. Not "Son of God" by actions alone. God himself, but also "true man" sharing misfortunes, actually suffering on Cross. Sounds contradiction, but message precisely **God became man**. Not "demigod" half-man/half-god (widespread Greek/Hellenistic belief). Formula: **"perfect God, perfect man."**

## 8. Postscript: Meeting of Two Cultures

- Christianity entering Greco-Roman world = dramatic meeting of two cultures, one of history's great cultural revolutions.
- Stepping out of antiquity: ~1,000 years since early Greek philosophers; ahead Christian Middle Ages ~1,000 years.
- Goethe: *"he who cannot draw on three thousand years is living from hand to mouth."* Knowing historical roots = only way to become human, more than naked ape, avoid floating in vacuum.
