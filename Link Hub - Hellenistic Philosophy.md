## The Schools (sections inside the overview)

- **Cynics** — Socratic simplicity pushed to provocation; the barrel, shamelessness, nature against convention.
- **Stoics** — Logos determinism, duty, apatheia; Heraclitean fire become world-reason.
- **Epicureans** — atoms and the Garden; pleasure as absence of disturbance; "live in seclusion."
- **Neoplatonism** — Plato fused with mysticism: Plotinus, the One, emanation, the soul as spark from the fire.
- **Mysticism (general doctrine)** — merging with God or cosmic spirit, East and West; purification and enlightenment.
